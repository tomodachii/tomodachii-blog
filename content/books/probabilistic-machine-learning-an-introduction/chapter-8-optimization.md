# Introduction
**Find** $ \theta \in \Theta $

**Minimizing** loss function $ L: \Theta \to R $

$$
\theta^\* \in \argmin_{\theta \in \Theta} L(\theta)
$$

* parameter space: $ \Theta \subseteq R^D $
* $ D $: \# of variables being optimized over.
